### Portfolio Analysis

#### https://uncc.instructure.com/eportfolios/2642/Home
#### The first portfolio comes from Spring 2022, authored by Mr Aidan Brendle. The website is straightforward, all assignments are readily found, and it retains a professional tone throughout. Decisions are supported by reasoning, relevant calculations, and weighted criteria, although the conclusions and lessons learned remain somewhat ambiguous. Since all decisions, calculations, and engineering drawings are included within the portfolio, the entire design process can be easily reproduced by a colleague. Overall, the portfolio is functional as a webpage for a student, but leaves room to be desired in the eyes of a potential employer, primarily due to tone. 


#### https://uncc.instructure.com/eportfolios/4602/home/about-me
#### This second portfolio, this time from Fall 2025 and authored by Neal Zalomek, leaves a sour initial impression, as the home page is non-functional. All links and assignments are present and can be navigated to, but not all are contained within the main site. Some pages are hyperlinks to external documents and webpages, or just prompt for a file download. With these added steps, parts of the portfolio may not be accessible to all users. All design decision are well articulated, include calculations, diagrams, modelling, and commentary on how the author arrived at those conclusions. Since all of the reasoning is included, a fellow engineer could quite readily reproduce the design as well as the process. Despite containing pronouns such as "I, me my", the document retains an appropriate, concise tone throughout, and remains appropriate for a document given to a potential employer.
